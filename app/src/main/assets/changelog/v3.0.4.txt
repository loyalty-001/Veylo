VEYLO CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 3.0.4
DATE: 2026-10-25

NEW

INSTALLATION
Install over the official Veylo app or the Operations test update. Your library and settings stay in place.

Analytics are optional. Veylo does not upload your viewing history, searches, or video identifiers.
