VEYLO CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 3.0.7
DATE: 2026-10-27
STATUS: stable

NEW
- Shape your music by choosing songs you enjoy from an artist, genre, or song search.
- Find a getting-started guide in Settings, also shown once after updating.
- Review categorized problem reports and optional error codes before sending.

IMPROVED
- Analytics default on after you choose Continue in the update notice. Existing opt-outs stay off, with delivery status and retry available.
- Admin Overview separates APK downloads, website clicks, and active installations, with data freshness and collection status.
- Recommendation feedback is easier to find in song menus.
- Download dialogs show save-folder guidance and where to find progress and retry.

FIXED
- Shorts use a complete outlined share arrow at the same size as neighboring actions, without a background.
- The Legal credits row uses a neutral subtitle while retaining original attribution inside its dialog.
- Website click delivery falls back when the browser cannot queue a beacon and still honors browser privacy signals.

INSTALLATION
Install over the official Veylo app. Your library and settings stay in place.
