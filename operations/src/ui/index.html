Service controls

Privacy and access

Admin access is restricted to the owner through Cloudflare Access and must have MFA enabled before it is unlocked.

App analytics use a random installation identifier, not an account or device identifier. Users can turn analytics off and request deletion of that identifier's activity. Daily activity expires after 30 days; first reported launches and submitted reports after 90 days; administrative audit entries after 180 days.

Public telemetry can be fabricated. Treat it as reported activity rather than verified unique people.
